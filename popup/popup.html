Acodax Excel Fill
  1. Open Add Purchase Invoice or Add Stock Transfer in Acodax.
  2. Fill the supplier, date and other header fields.
  3. Click Excel Fill (bottom right) and load your sheet, or paste the rows.
  4. Press Start filling, check the lines, then press Save yourself.

Sheet layout: column A = barcode, column B = quantity. An empty quantity counts as 1.
